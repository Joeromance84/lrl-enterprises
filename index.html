Lorentz Biotechnologies

Lorentz Biotechnologies develops topical formulations built on UDB v1.0, a delivery base we make and refine in-house. Our research question is simple to state and hard to answer: how well can a topical base carry ingredients into the skin, and can that be shown with real measurements?

We are at the preclinical stage. We make no claims about what our research formulations do in people. Those claims have to be earned through testing, in order, and that testing is what we are raising money for.

  1. Formulation development We are here

    The base and candidate formulas are made and refined in small batches, with batch records and mass-balance checks on every run.

  2. Franz cell permeation testing

    An independent lab measures how much of an ingredient actually passes into and through skin. This is the first objective evidence about the delivery base.

  3. Stability and preservative testing

    Shelf-life testing, plus preservative challenge testing (USP 51) to confirm the formula stays safe once it has been opened and used.

  4. GMP manufacturing

    Scale-up with a contract manufacturer working under current good manufacturing practice.

  5. Regulatory strategy

    A pre-IND meeting with the FDA to agree the path for any medicinal use before any study begins.

  6. Clinical studies

    Only under an active FDA IND and IRB approval, with informed consent from every participant.

Investment funds these steps in order, starting with Franz cell testing. We can share the detailed plan and budget directly. LRL Enterprises is registered on SAM.gov for federal grants and contracting.
